Nordwind raises interest with failure prediction for ageing wind fleets

Windpower Weekly, 12 March 2026

Hamburg-based Nordwind says its software can warn operators of gearbox failures weeks before they happen. The company now works with eight customers, most of them independent operators in northern Germany.

“Once the warranty ends, operators are on their own,” chief executive Lena Hartmann said. Nordwind charges per turbine and year.

Industry analysts note that failure prediction is becoming a priority as Europe’s first-generation turbines age.
