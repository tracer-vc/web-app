Why I think Nordwind could become the default for independent wind operators
Posted by a renewable-energy newsletter author, April 2026

I have been following Nordwind since their first pilots. My take: whoever collects the most labelled failure data across fleets will win this market, and Nordwind is ahead.

Operators I spoke to like that Sentinel works on top of the SCADA data they already have. Nobody wants another sensor installation project.

This is opinion, not investment advice.
